## 🛡️ SAFEGUARDS

### XP Caps (Prevent Grinding):
- Social posts: 15 XP/day, 50 XP/week
- Forum help: 30 XP/day, 150 XP/week
- Attendance: 20 XP/day, 100 XP/week
- Challenges: 100 XP/day, 300 XP/week

### Quality Thresholds:
- Social posts need 3+ upvotes to earn XP
- Forum answers need acceptance
- 60-minute cooldown between "help" XP

### XP Decay:
- 10% decay after 30 days inactive
- Encourages continued participation
- Admin can manually adjust

### Admin Controls:
- Revoke XP for abuse
- Adjust caps as needed
- Review manual challenge submissions
- Lock team changes if needed
