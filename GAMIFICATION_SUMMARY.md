## OPTION 1: XP & Level System ⭐ (RECOMMENDED START)

**How it works:**
- Earn XP for activities (attendance: 10XP, posts: 5XP, projects: 50XP, helping: 15XP)
- Level up: Novice → Explorer → Builder → Innovator → Expert → Master
- Display level on profile, leaderboard

**Best for:** Individual progression, clear goals

---

## OPTION 2: Challenges & Quests 🏆

**How it works:**
- Weekly challenges (e.g., "Write 500 lines of code")
- Multi-week quests (e.g., "Build a full app in 4 weeks")
- Earn badges and rewards

**Best for:** Engagement, short-term motivation

---

## OPTION 3: Team Competition 👥

**How it works:**
- 4 teams (P-R-I-S) compete termly
- Points for attendance, projects, wins
- Live leaderboard, termly awards

**Best for:** Community building, collaboration
