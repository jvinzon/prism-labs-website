# PRISM Labs Gamification - Design Consultation

You chose a HYBRID SYSTEM (all 3 approaches combined). Great choice!

Before I build it, I need your input on these key questions:

---

## THE HYBRID MODEL

```
INDIVIDUAL LAYER (XP + Levels)
  - Earn XP from activities
  - Level up over time
  - Personal badges

ENGAGEMENT LAYER (Challenges & Quests)
  - Weekly challenges -> Bonus XP
  - Multi-week quests -> Special badges
  - Limited events -> Rare rewards

COMMUNITY LAYER (Team Competition)
  - Team points (separate or combined?)
  - Termly leaderboard
  - Team + individual rewards
```

## QUESTION 1: XP VALUES

Suggested XP earnings:

**Individual:**
- Attend session: 10 XP
- Post on social: 5 XP (cap at 50 XP/week?)
- Complete milestone: 25-50 XP
- Help someone (accepted answer): 15 XP
- Weekly challenge: 30-50 XP bonus
- Quest completion: 100-200 XP
- Win competition: 100 XP
- Perfect attendance (4 weeks): 40 XP
- Mentor session: 25 XP

**Team:**
- Member attends: 5 team pts + 10 individual XP
- Project complete: 20 team pts + 50 individual XP
- Team wins: 50 team pts + 100 XP each

**QUESTIONS:**
- Are these values right? Too much/little?
- Need daily/weekly caps to prevent spam?
- Team points separate or combined with individual?

## QUESTION 2: LEVELS

Proposed 6 levels:

| Level | Name | XP Needed | Perks |
|-------|------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Novice | 0-99 | Basic |

**QUESTIONS:**

## QUESTION 3: TEAMS

Which structure?

**Option A: PRISM Tracks**
- Team P (Programming) - Blue
- Team R (Refurbishing) - Purple
- Team I (Innovation) - Green
- Team S (Systems) - Orange
- Pros: Aligns with club
- Cons: May be imbalanced

**Option B: Mixed Houses**
- 4 houses with random assignment
- Named after tech pioneers (Turing, Hopper, Lovelace, Berners-Lee)
- Pros: Balanced, cross-track
- Cons: Less track identity

**Option C: Student Choice**
- Students pick team
- Can change each term
- Pros: Fun, agency
- Cons: Imbalanced

**QUESTIONS:**
- Which option (A, B, or C)?
- Fixed for term or flexible?
- Reset points each term?
- Winning team rewards? (Trophy, pizza party?)

## MY RECOMMENDATIONS

1. XP: Start generous, cap social at 50 XP/week
2. Levels: 6 levels as proposed, visual badges
3. Teams: Option A (PRISM tracks) - aligns with club
4. Challenges: 3-5/week, mix auto/manual
5. Leaderboards: Members-only, top 10, opt-out
6. Rewards: Digital + privileges first, physical if budget
7. Cultural: Collaboration emphasis, consider Te Reo names
8. Phases: Build 1+2 first, test, then add 3
9. Anti-gaming: Caps + quality + admin oversight
10. Integration: Key features only, not everything
