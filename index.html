About PRISM Labs

PRISM Labs is the evolution of the ASDAH AI Club. We're a student-led technology lab where members explore AI, hardware, software, and systems management through real projects that serve our school community.

Open to Years 9-13, no experience required, free to join, and we use entirely free and open-source tools. Whether you're curious about programming, want to build AI projects, interested in hardware refurbishment, or excited about managing systems and infrastructure — there's a place for you here.

Four Tracks. One Lab.

P

Programming

Learn to build software that solves real problems. Python, web development, APIs, and the tools behind every modern app. From your first script to a deployed project.

R

Refurbishing

Give retired laptops a second life. Diagnose, repair, and rebuild school hardware — then deploy it for students who need it. Real hardware skills, real community impact.

I

Innovation

Explore AI, machine learning, and creative technology. Build smart tools that serve our school — from an AI-powered library search to automated media production. Where ideas become prototypes.

S

Systems Management

Run the infrastructure. IoT devices, servers, networks, and the systems that keep everything working. Build and deploy the ESP32 attendance system and manage the club's tech backbone.

What We're Building Toward

Smart Attendance System

Tap-in attendance using ESP32 RFID nodes built by students. Real hardware, real firmware, real impact on how our school tracks presence.

Coming Term 4 2026

Laptop Refurbishment Programme

Retired school laptops diagnosed, repaired, and redeployed by students. Reducing e-waste and bridging the digital divide.

Coming Term 4 2026

Smart Library Search

An AI-powered search that helps students find books by concept, reading level, or theme — not just keywords.

Coming 2027

Media Automation Hub

Automated highlight clips, player cards, and social media content for school sports teams and the brass band. From raw footage to polished content.

Coming 2027

Student Business Marketplace

A digital marketplace for student-run micro-businesses, gated by Positive Behaviour for Learning (PB4L) achievements. Real entrepreneurship, real responsibility.

Coming 2027

Campus Pulse Platform

The long-term vision: all of the above, packaged into a platform that other schools can use. Students building software that serves their community and beyond.
